## Paper Under Review

**PGCF: Physiology-Guided Consistency Framework for Deepfake Detection via Multi-Stream Temporal Attention**

- Three-stream architecture: spatial (ResNet50 + CBAM), frequency-domain, and rPPG (remote photoplethysmography) signals.
- Key contribution: Physiology-Guided Cross-Modal Consistency (PGCMC) module — gates multi-stream fusion using rPPG-derived spectral entropy as a confidence signal.
- Temporal modeling: Multi-Head Self-Attention → BiLSTM.
- Results: 356 clips (178 real / 178 fake), AUC 0.8240, accuracy 74.16%, AP 0.7907. Real vs. fake mean consistency: 0.690 vs. 0.272. Robust to Gaussian noise up to σ=0.20 (AUC degrades gracefully to 0.73).

## Verdict: Not a good fit — answered "No"

**Reasoning:**
- The paper is a computer-vision / multimedia-forensics paper (video deepfake detection). It has no RF, signal-transmission, channel, or network-hardware angle.
- No track matches directly. The closest candidates were ruled out:
  - Track 2 "ML for Communications" — means ML applied to communication systems (channel estimation, spectrum sensing, network optimization), not video/biometric forensics.
  - Track 2 "Cyber Security and Cryptography" — means network/crypto security, not media forensics or deepfake/spoofing detection.
  - Track 4 "Speech, Audio and Language Processing" — paper is video-based (spatial/frequency/rPPG), not speech/audio.
- Risk flagged: reviewers assembled around RF/comms/defense hardware would likely desk-reject on scope grounds regardless of technical quality.

**Recommendation given:** Look instead at deepfake/media-forensics venues, biometrics/security conferences (e.g., IEEE WIFS, IJCB/BTAS-adjacent), rPPG/affective-computing venues, or general AI/CV/multimedia conferences with a trustworthy-AI or security track.
